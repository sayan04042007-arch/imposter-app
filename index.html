📋 MISSION BRIEFING & RULES
01 Pass the Phone: Each player privately taps to see their secret word. Nobody knows their own role!
02 🚀 Crew: Receives the shared real word.
🔪 Impostor: Secretly gets a totally random word from a different category! No pattern!
03 Discussion: Each player gives ONE clue about their word. Listen for who sounds weirdly out of place!
04 🗳️ Every Player Votes: When discussion ends, pass the phone around so each and every player privately casts their vote for who they suspect!
05 👑 Who Wins?
• Right Voters Win: Any player who correctly votes for the real Impostor WINS the round! 🎯
• Impostor Wins: If the Impostor escapes getting majority votes, the Impostor wins! 😈
• Jester Wins: (4+ players) If the Jester tricks everyone into voting them out! 🤡
06 🚀 Airlock Ejection: The player with the most votes is launched into deep space with full warp-speed visual effects!
